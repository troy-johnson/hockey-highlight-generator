# Game-Sheet-First Selection with Precision-First Fill

Supersedes [ADR-0002](0002-recall-optimized-culling-and-reserves.md). Selection no longer feeds a human Verify Timeline. The Game Sheet decides which goals exist. The video only locates each goal's moment, and non-goal plays fill the Recap only when they are likely worth keeping.

**Context:** ADR-0002 assumed that a human watched every selected clip, so a junk clip cost one keystroke. The pipeline now publishes after one review: a data review, then a watch of the finished Recap. A junk play in the Recap is visible to viewers, and a missed save is not. The real-footage test (Ghost Pirates, 2025-11-09, measured against the user's published edit) found that, with correct camera sync, optical-flow events contain 12 of 15 goals, but their scores rank goals from 8th to 117th of 124 events. Flow score alone cannot choose plays. The stock HockeyAI model finds the goal frame on every frame but finds the puck in only 18% of frames near goals.

**Decision:**
- **Goals:** every Scoresheet goal is a goal. Its period, clock time, team, and the end-switch rule give a search window at one net. Inside the window, candidates are scored on a goal signature: a flow peak at that net, a whistle a few seconds later, a long stoppage, and a faceoff at center ice next. "Puck inside the goal frame" is an optional cue, because the puck often bounces back out. The first version uses a weighted rule, and a learned model replaces it once enough labeled games exist. A goal with no convincing moment still counts on the scorebug and is flagged "no clip found". Video-only goal finding is a fallback for a missing or unreadable Scoresheet.
- **Non-goal plays:** plays at the net (chances, saves, near misses, defensive stops) are automatic and ranked by one interest score. A penalty's infraction clip is automatic on a confident Game Sheet time match with a whistle or stoppage just after it. Hits and fights are suggested but not auto-included until a model trained on review labels reaches the precision target.
- **Interest score:** one score drives goal cutting, the non-goal fill, the best-goal full treatment, the cold-open pick, and the Short. For goals, game context comes first (game-winner, tying goal, overtime or shootout winner, last 2 minutes, Focus Team), then visual intensity. Non-goal plays use visual intensity only.
- **Targets:** every sheet goal has a clip or a "no clip found" flag. At least 90% of goal clips place the goal moment correctly without correction. At least 80% of auto-included non-goal plays are worth keeping.
- **Next Best list:** a short list of next-best plays replaces the Reserves timeline. The review page's "promote" draws from it.
- **Shootouts (about one a season):** when the sheet records a shootout, the pipeline looks after the last period for single-skater attempts at one net with pauses between them. It includes all attempts in order, uses the winner from the sheet, and lists the attempts for review.

**Considered:** Keeping ADR-0002's recall-first culling with a Reserves timeline. Rejected: there is no Verify Timeline to cull, and junk now reaches viewers. Puck-based goal detection with the stock HockeyAI model. Rejected: its puck recall near goals (18%) is far below the 70% bar.

**Consequences:** Review corrections, and each play's Play Type, become the training labels for the goal-signature model and the hit, fight, and defense models. Every change to Selection is measured against answer keys built from the user's published Recaps (EDL, raw footage, and frame matching). The user expects 10–15 such games.
